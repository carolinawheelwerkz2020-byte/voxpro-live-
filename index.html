Setup & tips
  1. Open iPhone Settings → Bluetooth and pair your speaker for the room mix.
  2. Come back here and tap Go Live — sound follows automatically.
  3. Keep this app in the foreground while you sing.
Bluetooth adds ~200ms of delay, so the singer should monitor through wired earbuds or the phone speaker — not the Bluetooth speaker. The room won't notice the delay.
  • Monitoring through the phone speaker with the mic open can squeal — keep the volume moderate or use earbuds.
  • For music, mic noise-cancellation is off on purpose. Find a quiet-ish spot.
  • Hold the phone 6–12 inches from your mouth, slightly off-axis.
